FAQ

Good questions, plainly answered.

Do you work with businesses as well as homes?

Both. We do residential installs — home Wi-Fi, backups, cameras and personal storage — and commercial work, from small offices to whole companies: business networks, file servers, archiving, monitored backups and locally-hosted data. Same careful standard either way.

What does “locally-hosted data” actually mean?

Instead of renting space in someone else’s cloud, we set up storage and services that live on hardware you own — in your home or office. Your files stay with you: fast, private, and with no monthly cloud bills.

Can you build custom tools, or just install hardware?

Both. Alongside networks, storage and backups, we build custom apps and automations — file movers, dashboards, integrations and small tools — shaped around your exact process, plus practical AI where it genuinely saves time.

Do you handle big archives and workstations?

Yes. We set up LTO tape archiving for large libraries that need to last years, and we build custom render and AI workstations — the right CPU, GPU and storage for the job, quiet and reliable.

Where are you based, and who do you cover?

Hackney, East London. We cover homes and businesses across the borough and nearby. Not sure if you’re in range? Just ask.
