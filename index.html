FAQ

Good questions, plainly answered.

Do you work with businesses as well as homes?

Both. We do residential installs — home Wi-Fi, backups, cameras and personal storage — and commercial work, from small offices to whole companies: business networks, file servers, monitored backups and locally-hosted data. Same careful standard either way.

What does “locally-hosted data” actually mean?

Instead of renting space in someone else’s cloud, we set up storage and services that live on hardware you own — in your home or office. Your files stay with you: fast, private, and with no monthly cloud bills.

Can you handle backups and recovery?

Yes. We set up automated backups — local and off-site — monitor that they’re running, and test that they actually restore, so nothing is lost when a drive eventually fails.

What about hard drives and NAS storage?

We supply, install and configure drives and NAS units — from a single home backup drive to multi-bay business storage with redundancy, so a failed disk never means lost data.

Where are you based, and who do you cover?

Hackney, East London. We cover homes and businesses across the borough and nearby. Not sure if you’re in range? Just ask.
