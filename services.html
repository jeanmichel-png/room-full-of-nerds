Networking & Wi-Fi

Whole-home and whole-office mesh, wired backbones, VLANs and guest networks. We survey, cable and configure so every corner is fast and nothing drops.

Data & Storage

The right drives, RAID and NAS (Synology and beyond) for your workload — from a single home backup disk to multi-terabyte arrays with redundancy, so a dead disk is never a lost file.

Backups & Recovery

A proper 3-2-1 backup: local, off-site and monitored. We test the restores on a schedule, so your data actually comes back when it matters.

Locally-Hosted Data

Your own private cloud on hardware you own — file sync, shared drives and services in-house. Fast, private, and no monthly cloud bills.

LTO & Long-Term Archiving

Long-term LTO-9 tape archive for anyone sitting on years of files — accounts, records, projects or media — with a clear index so it stays findable and safe long after hard drives fail.

Workstations & GPU Builds

Custom PCs and workstations for creators, analysts, gamers and AI — RTX-class GPUs and the right CPU and storage for the job, built quiet and reliable, supported locally.

Security & Cameras

Discreet CCTV, access and alerts for homes and premises — installed tidily and set up so you can actually check them from your phone.

Custom Apps & Automation

Bespoke software that removes the busywork — native macOS apps, web apps (think a full quoting system on Next.js + Supabase), dashboards, integrations, plugins and automations, all shaped around your exact process.

AI Systems & Strategy

Practical AI woven into real work — automation plus scrape-and-tag pipelines that sort thousands of items, set up safely and explained in plain English.
