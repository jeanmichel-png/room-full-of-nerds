About

A small team of big nerds.

Production-grade technical roots, pointed at everyday problems.

We come from high-end London production and post — where deadlines are brutal and the tech simply has to work: multi-terabyte RAID and NAS moving files around the building, render machines, archives measured in years, and automation that saves hours a day.

Room Full of Nerds brings that same rigour to homes and local businesses. Clean, reliable systems, real human support, and plain English always — no jargon, no mystery, no being made to feel daft.

Built in-house

Systems most people never see

  • A native macOS app that moves terabytes between drives — safely, with verified receipts
  • Multi-bay RAID and NAS storage, with backups we actually test
  • LTO tape archives that outlast any hard drive
  • RTX-class render and AI workstations
  • Data tools that scrape, tag and organise thousands of items automatically
  • Web apps and internal tools — quoting systems, dashboards and client portals
